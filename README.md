# Pick a Buddhist text off the shelf.

Every text on the shelf, standing in the century it was written. Pull one out to read it.

A text whose English is published is on the shelf and opens where it stands; one still to come stands as a book to come, dated from its sources, until its translation is published.

## Second century

- [The Abhidharma Mahāvibhāṣā](README.md). The five hundred arhats of Kashmir, translated into Chinese by Xuanzang. Chinese. Coming; not published yet. Written: 150–250 CE. Edition: Takakusu Junjirō and Watanabe Kaigyoku, 1924–1934. Length: about 200 fascicles, about 1,480,000 characters of Chinese.
